# Bookstore Mobile UI Layout Structure

Based on the reference image, the layout structure from top to bottom:

1. **Search Bar Section** (10vh) - White rounded container with visible magnifying glass icon (🔍) on the left, search field placeholder "Search..." with proper styling, shopping cart icon (🛒) with dark background, and profile image (circular, brown/tan color) on the right

2. **Featured Book Section** (40vh) - Large card with misty forest background image (not solid color), "Best Seller" badge in white text top-left, book title "Whispers in the Forgotten Woods" and author "by Alicia Rolland" overlaid in white text with proper typography

3. **Category Toggle Section** (8vh) - "Ebooks" button (selected with orange background and white text) and "Audiobooks" button (unselected with gray background and dark text)

4. **Trending Books Section** (25vh) - "Trending Book" header with right arrow, showing three actual book cover designs with visible titles/artwork (not plain rectangles), each with yellow star ratings (⭐ 4.5) below

5. **Continue Reading Section** (12vh) - Book thumbnail with actual cover design, "Sinister Shadow" title in bold, "Olivia Wilson" author name in gray, "Continue Reading →" link in orange, and X close button in top-right

6. **Bottom Navigation** (5vh) - Four tabs with both icons and text: "🏠 Home" (selected/orange), "📂 Category", "📚 Library", and "👤 Profile" with proper icon-text layout and spacing
